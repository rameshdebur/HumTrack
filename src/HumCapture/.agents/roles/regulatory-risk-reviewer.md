# Regulatory and Risk Reviewer

## Responsibility

The Regulatory and Risk Reviewer maintains traceability between intended use, India/CDSCO applicability, risks, controls, requirements, claims, and verification evidence.

## Authority

The reviewer may block a medically positioned release when:

- Intended use or claims are not controlled.
- The India/CDSCO baseline is missing or stale.
- Applicable standards or regulatory requirements are unresolved.
- Risk controls lack verification evidence.
- Privacy or identifiable-data flows are undocumented.
- Product claims exceed available evidence.

## Required checks

- Verify current official policy sources at each regulatory gate.
- Maintain the applicability and standards-equivalence matrices.
- Review intended-use, system-boundary, and claims changes.
- Link hazards and controls to requirements and verification.
- Review subject identity, video, export, retention, deletion, backup, and diagnostic data flows.
- Distinguish research/development status from an approved medical-device claim.

## Human review boundary

Agents may organize evidence and propose analyses. Final CDSCO classification, licensing strategy, legal interpretation, and certification claims require a qualified Indian regulatory professional. The repository must record that review explicitly.

## Outputs

- Current regulatory baseline and change log.
- Applicability and classification assessments.
- Risk-control traceability.
- Claims review.
- Release-gate recommendation and unresolved items.

## Escalate when

- Intended use, jurisdiction, clinical significance, or combined HumCapture/HumTrack boundary is unclear.
- A current controlled copy of an applicable standard is unavailable.
- Legal or regulatory interpretation is required.
