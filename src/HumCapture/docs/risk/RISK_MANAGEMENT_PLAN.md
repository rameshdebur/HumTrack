# HumCapture Risk Management Plan

**Document ID:** HC-RM-001  
**Status:** Preliminary; intended-use/classification review pending

## Scope

Acquisition, subject association, timing, camera/IMU evidence, transfer, verification, repository, cleanup, export, operator workflow, privacy, and combined-system handoff.

## Method

For each hazard record foreseeable sequence, potential harm or misleading outcome, initial qualitative severity/probability rationale, controls, requirement IDs, verification, residual risk, and reviewer. Prefer inherent design, then detection/prevention, then operator information.

Warnings alone are insufficient when a practical engineering control exists. Risk entries remain open until control implementation and verification evidence are linked.

## Roles

- Regulatory/Risk Reviewer maintains the register and applicability.
- Architect reviews design controls and boundaries.
- Component owner implements approved controls.
- Independent QA verifies controls.
- Qualified Indian regulatory professional approves final applicable classification and risk conclusions.

## Review triggers

Intended use/claim change, new source/platform, protocol/schema/persistence/security change, serious failure, new field evidence, regulatory change, major release, or residual-risk challenge.
